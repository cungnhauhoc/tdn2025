### 📖 READING (16 points)

Read the following passage and answer the questions.  

**Teenagers need sleep!**  
Sleep is very important for everyone, but it is especially important for teenagers. Teenagers are going through a lot of changes, both physically and emotionally. These changes can make it difficult for them to get enough sleep. Most teenagers need about 8-10 hours of sleep each night. However, many teenagers don't get enough sleep. This can lead to many problems, such as:  
- Difficulty concentrating in school: When teenagers don't get enough sleep, they may have trouble paying attention in class and remembering what they have learned.  
- Mood problems: Lack of sleep can make teenagers feel irritable, anxious, or depressed.  
- Health problems: Over time, lack of sleep can lead to more serious health problems, such as obesity, diabetes, and heart disease.  
There are many reasons why teenagers don't get enough sleep. Some teenagers have trouble falling asleep. Others may have to stay up late to study or work. And some teenagers may simply not make sleep a priority. It is important for teenagers to make sleep a priority. Here are some tips to help teenagers get enough sleep:  
- Go to bed and wake up at the same time each day, even on weekends.  
- Create a relaxing bedtime routine, such as taking a warm bath or reading a book.  
- Make sure your bedroom is dark, quiet, and cool.  
- Avoid caffeine and alcohol before bed.  
- Get regular exercise, but not too close to bedtime.
